RECOMMENDED — P310 RECOVERY, native fw v0.1.5 (ESP32, 4 MB)

The fix build after the dead-panel incident: a stalled display update can never reboot the panel in a loop again (no relay self-off), Display restart returns the screen from the splash logo, and the bootloader rolls a failed update back to the previous firmware. Ships the PROVEN display content and the boot power hardening (capped radio TX power, the chip's own reset-reason report on the serial console, AND the setup form fixed — it saves again after the security guard had locked it out).

Wi-Fi + MQTT settings are PRESERVED — this flash does not touch NVS. After the flash the panel joins Wi-Fi/MQTT on its own and pulls its current firmware/checksums from NSPanel Core automatically.

Chrome or Edge (Web Serial) with a 3.3 V USB-TTL adapter wired to TX/RX/GND. Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT — then click INSTALL. Do NOT erase the flash when prompted. Afterwards tap RESET once and power the panel from mains.

NSPanel Core — panel recovery

  1. Unplug the panel (mains OFF), open it, connect a USB-to-TTL adapter (3.3V): GND→GND, TX→RX, RX→TX.
  2. Put the ESP32-C6 into download mode (hold BOOT, tap RESET).
  3. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (needs WebSerial). Click install, pick the USB serial port, confirm the erase when asked.

No software to install, no commands to type. This writes bootloader, partition table, firmware 0.1.177 and the blank setup page.

After the install: the panel restarts on its own. That is where this page ends — what happens next with the screen is your call, not an instruction from here.

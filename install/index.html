NSPanel Core native firmware line

RECOMMENDED — native fw v0.1.0 (ESP32, 4 MB)

The fixed native build: display handshake works (UART pins corrected), temperature reads the real NTC, OTA streams to flash and self-configures the manager address from the register_accept.

WiFi + MQTT settings are PRESERVED — this flash does not touch NVS. After the flash the panel joins WiFi/MQTT on its own; nothing to re-enter.

Chrome or Edge (Web Serial) with a 3.3 V USB-TTL adapter wired to TX/RX/GND. Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT — then click INSTALL. Do NOT erase the flash when prompted (the button above does not erase). Afterwards tap RESET once.

Rollback — the OLD firmware line (v0.0.1, full image)

Flashes the complete old-line image (bootloader + partitions + app + config filesystem) at 0x0. Erases everything — after this the panel starts its own setup AP (NSPanelCore, then http://192.168.1.1) and WiFi/MQTT must be entered again.

Wired flash alternative (no browser): the general nspanel-core-native-0.1.0-esp32-wired-flash.zip on the fw-v0.1.0 release contains the same files + an esptool command line.

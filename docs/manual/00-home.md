# NSPanel Core — the user manual

NSPanel Core replaces the stock NSPanel firmware with a native ESP32
application and turns the panel into a Home-Assistant-driven wall display:
your own dashboard pages, a screensaver with your photo and the weather,
and physical switches that do exactly what you configure.

This manual walks through everything in the order you'll use it:

1. **Installation** — get the integration into Home Assistant
2. **First run** — point the manager at your broker
3. **Accept a panel** — the panel joins over MQTT
4. **Panel settings** — the modes, the relays, the screensaver timeout
5. **The Dashboard studio** — design your pages and send them to the glass
6. **The screensaver** — the style, the photo, the weather
7. **The panel's own web UI** — status, actions, recovery
8. **Updates** — how firmware and displays update themselves
9. **Troubleshooting** — the common issues and the fixes

The screenshots show one example setup; your names, entities and photos
will be your own.
